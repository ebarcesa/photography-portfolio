Why I Love Shooting on Film

April 2025

Film photography slows you down. It makes you think — every shot is intentional, and every imperfection tells a story. The texture, the light leaks, the grain… it’s magic. This also tests your patience since you will need to wait until the roll of fim is used then that's when you can view all the photos you captured which will take weeks,months, or even a year

Capturing Real Moments

March 2025

Poses are cute, but raw, candid moments? Those are unforgettable. Whether it's a tear, laughter, or someone mid-blink, that’s where the story lives.

Tips for Beginners

February 2025

Start with your phone or a thrifted camera. Don’t stress about getting the most advanced or trendy camera. Learn to observe light, frame your shots, and most importantly, shoot often. You'll improve with every click.
