About Me

Hi! I'm a passionate photographer who finds beauty in simplicity and emotion. My journey started with a disposable camera and turned into a full-blown obsession with storytelling through both film and digital lenses.

I like captioning raw, unfiltered moments. The candid smiles, fleeting glances, and imperfect memories. My style embraces authenticity. I'm a sucker for memories, and I believe photography is the most beautiful way to preserve them forever.
